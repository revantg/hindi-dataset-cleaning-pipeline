Synthetic data works as an amplifier right up until the point where it collapses

High-quality real text is finite, and the modern answer to that scarcity is that we rephrase, recaption and re-style what we already have into fresh synthetic tokens, which lifts quality at a fixed token budget when we do it carefully while it begins to eat its own tail once we push the same seed too far. As we generate variants from one scarce seed and then set the real / synthetic mix, we can watch quality rise toward a peak and then fall away.
