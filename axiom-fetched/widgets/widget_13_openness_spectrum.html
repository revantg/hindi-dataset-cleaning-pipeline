"Open" is two dials, not one

Architecture has become essentially commoditized while the real moat now lives in the data, which is why a model can release its weights to the world while keeping its corpus a secret, and most "open" models behave in exactly this way. We can read this plane along two axes at once, since the horizontal direction measures weights openness and the vertical direction measures data openness. The top-right corner, where the weights and the data and the recipe have all been made public, remains almost entirely empty. As we click a model we can see what it actually released, and by dragging the violet V5 marker we can decide where this cohort commits to sit.
