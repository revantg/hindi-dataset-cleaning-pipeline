The V4 corpus, dissected

What we are looking at is the actual pretraining corpus that this team built and trained 120B / 9B / 5B / 2B on, which came to 1,118B tokens across 33,353 shards organised into three tiers. As we select a pool we can read its real numbers, and as we drag the growth stage the mixture rebalances in front of us, while flipping the OPUS lens reveals why a third of the corpus was deliberately hidden from the selector.

The whole design turns on one fact, which is that the golden proxy is English-heavy (cosine 0.876 with the web band), so OPUS systematically under-values Indic data and would reject it if we let it. Because Always-ON guarantees that every Indic script trains at every stage, we can now see why a third of the corpus bypasses the selector. 1,118B trained ⇄ ~1,254B effective
