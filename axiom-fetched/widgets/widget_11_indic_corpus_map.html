When the headline tokens are far larger than the real tokens we can train on

Indic corpora tend to look considerably bigger than what they really contain, and Sangraha is a good illustration of this because it ships 251B tokens across 22 languages while roughly 65% of that count is synthetic material that was machine-translated and transliterated out of English. What the verified split reveals to us is the genuine scarcity underneath these numbers, since Telugu's headline of 16.3B collapses down to 3.7B verified once we remove the padding, and Odia's 12.5B falls all the way to 1.2B. As we flip the toggle to collapse the synthetic padding, we can watch the real corpus emerge in front of us.
