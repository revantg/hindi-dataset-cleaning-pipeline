Token budget & scaling explorer

When we hold a compute budget fixed, Chinchilla tells us that training on roughly 20 tokens per parameter sits on the compute-optimal frontier, and once we begin to weigh inference cost we start over-training a smaller model on far more tokens, which is how Llama 3 8B ended up seeing about 1,900 tokens/param. As we slide the two dials we can watch the dot travel across the log-log plane, and by dragging the V5 marker we can feel how the regime shifts underneath us.

As we drag V5 from the Chinchilla point across to the Llama-3 point the tokens/param multiplier explodes, and even though the compute philosophy stays the same the appetite for data grows enormously. That extra data is precisely the labor this course exists to teach.
The Chinchilla ~20 tokens/param frontier and every tokens/param readout are computed live in JS from the slider values, and 32 real models are plotted here. All but one of them carry an officially disclosed pretraining-token count taken from tech reports and model cards, which covers GPT-3 and Chinchilla as the canonical anchors, DeepSeek V2/V3, Qwen2.5 and Qwen3, Gemma 2 and 3, Llama 4 Scout and Maverick, Phi-3.5/Phi-4, Nemotron-4, Granite 3.0, OLMo 2, SmolLM2, Falcon 3, GLM-4.5, Kimi K2, along with Sarvam 1 / 30B / 105B. MoE models are plotted on their total params, and Falcon 3 10B shows cumulative exposure of 14T inherited plus 2T continued. Mistral Large 2 carries a dashed ring because Mistral does not disclose its token count, so the ~15T figure is an estimate that we flag in its tooltip. Any model for which we could find no defensible figure, such as MiniMax-01, Command A, and Yi-Lightning, we have omitted rather than guessed. ~20 = optimal · far above = over-trained
