03 / what the run tells us

Results with
context.

These are the conclusions supported by the recorded run — and the conclusions it deliberately does not support.

Highest agreement

Residual + layout controls

Residual markup removal and layout-preserving rendering both reached 96.15% mean reference overlap. Layout rendering retained more material: 19.379% of raw HTML versus 9.276%.

Library behavior

Inscriptis keeps shape

Inscriptis retained 9,546.8 mean characters and reached 95.92% overlap. That is evidence of layout-preserving output, not proof that all retained boilerplate is useful.

Main-content trade-off

Shorter is not automatically better

Trafilatura, Resiliparse, jusText, and Readability produced compact candidates with overlap from 62.97% to 75.25%. Their output must be reviewed for omission and boilerplate, not selected by length alone.
