Save the best data for last

Modern pretraining runs in two stages, where the first stage burns through trillions of mostly-web tokens and then, in the final slice, we cool the learning rate down while the model trains on a small, heavily-filtered "anneal" mix that blends math, code, and high-quality web. That cooldown buys us a large benchmark jump whenever we have reserved our best data back at collection time. As we compose the mix below, we can watch the lift respond.

The published result from OLMo 2 (Ai2, 2025)
OLMo 2's two-stage recipe worked as follows. Stage 1 trained the 7B on ~3.9T tokens, >95% web (OLMo 2 Mix 1124). Stage 2 was a learning-rate cooldown on Dolmino Mix 1124, which was more heavily filtered and carried partly synthetic math, subsampled to 50B / 100B / 300B (the full mix is 843B tokens). The 7B averages a 50B-token sample run three times with checkpoint averaging. Although the anneal reserve is small next to the 3.9T bulk, it still delivers a +10.6 average benchmark lift.
