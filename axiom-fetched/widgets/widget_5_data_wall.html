The data wall

High-quality public text is finite, and here we are watching two curves at once, where one traces the stock of usable tokens on the open web while the other traces how many tokens a frontier training run wants as that appetite climbs quickly. The point where they cross is what we call the wall, the moment when simply scraping more of the web stops carrying us forward, and as we turn on the three escapes we can watch that crossing slide later in time. All dates are projections.

This is exactly why V5 moves beyond the idea of scraping ever more English web. The open high-quality stock is a closing window, so V5 leans on the levers that sit off that curve, which means synthetic amplification of scarce text together with Indic and multilingual data that the English-centric web never counted. V4 already lived this reality, since 162B of its Indic corpus was machine-translated synthetic and its Indic scripts were protected at every stage.
